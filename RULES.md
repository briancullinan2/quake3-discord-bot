1) I've linked this rule to the edit page to provide easy access to its diplomacy. [Links to source are a courtesy.](https://github.com/briancullinan/quake3-discord-bot/edit/main/RULES.md)
2) "Free thought" is not a moral high ground to disavow someone else's faith.
3) Knowledge is greater than power.
4) Time cannot be saved, it can only be micro-managed.
5) All transitions from dark to light or light to dark are met with empathy.
6) "Self-promotion" 100% welcome. We want people to share what they are happy about.
7) Misunderstanding is an excuse to break the rules. As long as you're willing to admit that's what happened.
8) Intellectual property will always be the property of the living author. Any sharing for technical reasons should be sanitized to no more than 10% of the original work or demonstrate written consent from the author.
9) "Genius is the ability to make something very complicated very simple." The mind is complicated, the simplest explanation is, you're a genius too.
10) People should have the right to be remembered and forgotten. Treat people with respect in regards to how they present themselves, from now on.
